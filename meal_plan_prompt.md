## Oil-free cooking notes

-   Steam idlis, puttu, idiyappam and vegetables.
-   Prepare dosa, adai and uthappam on a good non-stick or seasoned iron
    tawa without oil.
-   Dry-roast spices and use water-based cooking instead of oil-based
    tempering.
-   Make chutneys without oil.
-   Use coconut in moderate quantities.
-   Prefer boiled, steamed and pressure-cooked preparations.
-   Check packaged ingredients (such as semiya) for added oil where
    relevant.

## Nutrition and flexibility notes

-   Include protein-rich foods such as dal, chickpeas, green gram,
    cowpeas, roasted gram, curd and paneer regularly.
-   Include generous vegetable portions at lunch and dinner.
-   Aim for 1--2 servings of seasonal fruit daily.
-   Drink water regularly throughout the day.
-   These portions are starting points, not strict limits. Adjust them
    according to hunger, activity, and individual needs.
-   This is a general meal plan, not a medical diet. If you have a
    medical condition, specific nutritional needs, or food allergies,
    seek individualized advice from a qualified dietitian.

## Clarification

The plan assumes no known food allergies because none were specified.
Confirm allergies before using the plan. It interprets "avoid oil" as no
added cooking oil, including oil used for tempering or greasing.
